About Us

Car buying advice that starts with other buyers.

Auto Review Hub is a dealership review platform for the United States. Instead of relying on a dealer's own marketing, buyers read real customer reviews, see the sentiment behind them, and compare dealerships side by side before they visit a showroom.

Why this platform exists

A car is one of the biggest purchases most people make, yet the experience varies enormously from one dealership to the next: the quoted price, the honesty of the negotiation, the paperwork, the delivery. Those differences rarely show up in advertisements. They show up in reviews.

Auto Review Hub collects those reviews in one place and turns them into something a buyer can act on quickly: a reputation score for every dealer, a sentiment breakdown of their reviews, and tools to search, map and compare dealers before committing to a visit.

What you can do here

Dealer reputation scores

Every dealership card shows the share of its reviews that are positive, computed from real customer reviews.

Search, filter and map

Find dealers by name or city, filter by state, and see every dealer plotted on a map of the United States.

Side-by-side comparison

Compare up to three dealers on score, review volume, sentiment split and the cars they sell.

Reviews that help back

Post a review after your purchase, mark other reviews helpful, and find all your reviews in one place.

How the sentiment score works

1

A customer posts a review

The review is saved with the car bought, the purchase date and the dealership it belongs to.

2

The analyzer reads the words

A dedicated sentiment service scores the review text as positive, neutral or negative.

3

Scores roll up per dealer

Individual sentiments combine into each dealer's reputation score and sentiment breakdown.

Built with

Auto Review Hub is a full-stack application: a Django service for users and the web app, a React front end, an Express and MongoDB service for dealers, cars and reviews, and a Flask sentiment analyzer, all containerized with Docker.
